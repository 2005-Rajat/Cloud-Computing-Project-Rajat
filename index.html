Our Culinary Journey

Since 1991, Jaiveer Di Hatti has been synonymous with the authentic taste of Civil Lines, Ludhiana. What started as a small cart has blossomed into a beloved local landmark.

Our secret lies in our commitment to tradition. We still use the same family recipes, grinding our own spices and sourcing the freshest ingredients daily to bring you snacks that don't just satisfy your hunger, but warm your heart.
